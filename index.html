About Kristen

A path from nutrition science to media.

Kristen combines dietetics training with nutrition writing, recipe development, and on-camera experience.

Education

Psychology and dietetics

Kristen earned a BA in Psychology from the University of Arizona and a BS in Dietetics from Arizona State University.

Graduate training

Human nutrition

After her dietetic internship, Kristen completed an MS in Human Nutrition at the University of Western States.

Today

Nutrition communications

As owner of Camelback Nutrition & Wellness, Kristen helps brands communicate nutrition science through strategy, commentary, content, and spokesperson work.
